Neon Vice Cover Studio — turn any photo and caption into GTA 6-style cover art, with PNG export. Upload a photo or pick a sample, choose a layout and colour grade, pan and zoom each panel, then export a poster at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Fully offline: fonts vendored, no network calls at runtime.

Run: `node serve.ts` (serves the app at http://localhost:8080)
